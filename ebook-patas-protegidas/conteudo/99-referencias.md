# Referências {: style="font-size:22pt;margin:1mm 0 3mm" }

As orientações deste guia foram elaboradas a partir das fontes abaixo, consultadas em 2026. Recomendações veterinárias são revisadas periodicamente: em caso de dúvida, consulte a versão mais recente de cada documento e, sobretudo, o médico-veterinário que acompanha o seu cão.

### Diretrizes veterinárias internacionais

1. SQUIRES, R. A. et al. **2024 guidelines for the vaccination of dogs and cats** – compiled by the Vaccination Guidelines Group (VGG) of the World Small Animal Veterinary Association (WSAVA). *Journal of Small Animal Practice*, v. 65, p. 277-316, 2024. <https://wsava.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/2024-Guidelines-for-the-Vaccination-of-Dogs-and-Cats.pdf>
2. WSAVA – World Small Animal Veterinary Association. **Global Nutrition Guidelines** e **Global Nutrition Toolkit** (inclui escala de condição corporal e orientações para escolha de alimentos). <https://wsava.org/global-guidelines/global-nutrition-guidelines/>
3. WSAVA. **Body Condition Score – Dog** (escala de 9 pontos). <https://wsava.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/Body-Condition-Score-Dog.pdf>
4. NIEMIEC, B. et al. **World Small Animal Veterinary Association Global Dental Guidelines**. *Journal of Small Animal Practice*, v. 61, n. 7, 2020. <https://wsava.org/global-guidelines/global-dental-guidelines/>
5. CREEVY, K. E. et al. **2019 AAHA Canine Life Stage Guidelines**. *Journal of the American Animal Hospital Association*, v. 55, p. 267-290, 2019. <https://www.aaha.org/aaha-guidelines/life-stage-canine-2019/>
6. ELLIS, J. et al. **2022 AAHA Canine Vaccination Guidelines**. *Journal of the American Animal Hospital Association*, v. 58, p. 213-230, 2022. <https://www.aaha.org/aaha-guidelines/2022-aaha-canine-vaccination-guidelines/home/>
7. CLINE, M. G. et al. **2021 AAHA Nutrition and Weight Management Guidelines for Dogs and Cats**. *Journal of the American Animal Hospital Association*, v. 57, p. 153-178, 2021. <https://www.aaha.org/resources/2021-aaha-nutrition-and-weight-management-guidelines/>
8. QUIMBY, J. et al. **2023 AAHA Senior Care Guidelines for Dogs and Cats**. *Journal of the American Animal Hospital Association*, v. 59, p. 1-21, 2023. <https://www.aaha.org/resources/2023-aaha-senior-care-guidelines-for-dogs-and-cats/>
9. BURKITT-CREEDON, J. M. et al. **2024 RECOVER Guidelines: Updated treatment recommendations for CPR in dogs and cats**. *Journal of Veterinary Emergency and Critical Care*, v. 34, supl. 1, p. 104-123, 2024. <https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38924627/>
10. AVSAB – American Veterinary Society of Animal Behavior. **Position Statements** (Humane Dog Training, 2021; Puppy Socialization, 2008). <https://avsab.org/resources/position-statements/>
11. AVDC – American Veterinary Dental College. **Companion Animal Dental Scaling Without Anesthesia** – position statement. <https://avdc.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/AVDC-pos-stmts/Dental_Scaling_Without_Anesthesia.pdf>
